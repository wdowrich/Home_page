Executive Profile

Global insurance and retirement-services executive with 30+ years of leadership experience spanning CEO and C-suite management, life and retirement, reinsurance, capital and balance-sheet management, investments, actuarial, M&A and investment banking. Distinctive combination of enterprise leadership, deep insurance and actuarial expertise, public-company governance and capital-markets experience, with a track record of leading complex businesses, transformations and transactions across the U.S. and major international markets.

Currently Senior Executive Vice President & Chief Financial Officer of TIAA and Chair & Co-CEO of Seven30 Re, with leadership responsibilities spanning finance, actuarial, general account investments, strategic venture capital, reinsurance and balance-sheet solutions. Previously served as Interim CEO of AIG International Life & Retirement (now Corebridge) and CFO & Chief Financial Actuary of Institutional Markets, Life & Retirement (now Corebridge), providing direct leadership experience within businesses that today form part of Corebridge. Also served as CFO of Prudential Financial’s international businesses and held senior insurance capital-markets and risk-management leadership roles at Goldman Sachs.

Experienced public-company and financial-services board director, currently serving on the boards of Scotiabank and VantageRisk, with Audit, Risk and Corporate Governance committee experience. Proven ability to engage effectively with boards, investors, regulators and other critical stakeholders; build and lead high-performing teams; optimize capital and risk-adjusted returns; and translate complex financial, actuarial and strategic issues into decisive business actions and sustainable long-term value creation. Fellow of the Society of Actuaries with an MBA in Finance from The Wharton School.
